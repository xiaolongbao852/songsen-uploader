Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2, 2026.

Songsen Uploader is a personal tool used by its owner to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channel. It accesses only the owner's YouTube account, and only uploads videos the owner chooses to upload.

The tool does not collect, store, sell, or share personal data of any third party. It does not use analytics or advertising.

OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's own machine and are never shared with third parties.

Contact: via the Songsen channel page on YouTube.
